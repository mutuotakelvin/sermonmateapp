# Privacy Policy for SermonMate

**Last updated: 12 July 2026**

SermonMate ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is operated by **bobakdevs**. This Privacy
Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.
By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

If you have any questions, contact us at **info@bobakdevs.com**.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you register, we collect your **name** and **email
address**, and a securely managed password, through Google Firebase Authentication.

**Content you create.** We store the content you generate and save in the App,
including:
- **Reflections** you generate and choose to save (topic, verses, and text).
- **Mood check-ins** — the mood you select, the reasons you choose, and any note you add.

**Usage information.** To operate the free tier fairly, we keep a **daily count of AI
generations** per account. We do not use this for advertising or profiling.

**Subscription status.** If you subscribe to SermonMate Pro, we store whether your
subscription is active. Payment is processed by Google Play; **we never receive or
store your card, bank, or M-Pesa details.**

**We do not collect** your contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or
analytics tracking across other apps. The App shows no ads.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the App and your account.
- To **generate reflections and encouragement** using AI (see Section 3).
- To enforce free-tier limits and unlock Pro features for subscribers.
- To send you the **daily verse reminders** you enable (delivered as local
  notifications on your device).
- To respond to your support requests and account-deletion requests.

## 3. AI-Generated Content and Third Parties

When you request a reflection or mood encouragement, the **topic or mood details you
provide are sent to Anthropic (Claude)**, our AI provider, which generates the text.
We do not send your name or email with these requests. AI-generated content may be
imperfect and is provided for personal encouragement and study only.

We rely on the following service providers ("processors"), who handle data on our
behalf under their own privacy terms:

| Provider | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions) | Accounts, storing your reflections/moods, running the app backend |
| Anthropic (Claude) | Generating reflection and encouragement text |
| RevenueCat & Google Play Billing | Managing subscriptions and payments |
| Expo | App build and delivery infrastructure |

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Your data is stored on Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure and protected by industry
standard security and access rules. We retain your account and content until you
delete your account or ask us to remove your data. Subscription records are retained
as required for billing and legal purposes.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access / correction:** You can view and edit your reflections and moods in the App.
- **Deletion:** You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by
  emailing **info@bobakdevs.com** with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will
  process your request and delete your data.
- **Notifications:** You can turn daily verse reminders on or off in the App at any time.
- **Subscriptions:** You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play or via
  the App's subscription management screen.

## 6. Children's Privacy

SermonMate is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13
(or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information,
contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including
where our providers (Google, Anthropic, RevenueCat) operate. We take steps to ensure
your data is handled in line with this Policy wherever it is processed.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an
updated "Last updated" date, and where appropriate we will notify you in the App.
