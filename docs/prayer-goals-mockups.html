Prayer Goals — screen mockups

Proposed screens for the prayer routine feature. Colours are the real lib/theme.ts palette, so this is roughly what it will look like in the app. Toggle the theme top-right. Anything here can change — this is for review, not a build spec.

A new row on the home screen, sitting with the existing entries. Shows today's progress at a glance so the home screen answers "where am I today?" without a tap.

3The notification — one-tap logging

The friction reducer. Logging happens from the lock screen without opening the app, because a tracker you have to unlock for is a tracker people abandon.

13:00
Thursday, 30 July
SermonMate · now
Midday prayer
A few minutes, whenever you're ready.
I prayedPray with me
Notes
"I prayed" writes the log entry directly and dismisses. No app launch.
"Pray with me" opens the app and generates a short prayer — this is the only part of the feature that spends AI quota.
Copy is deliberately unpressured: "whenever you're ready", never "you're falling behind". Separate Android channel, so these can be muted without losing the daily verse.

4Logging with a note

Opening the app to log gives you the option to say what you prayed about. Tapping "Done" without typing is the fast path — the note is never required.

Note
The log is already written when this sheet appears — closing it without typing loses nothing. That ordering matters: never make the record conditional on finishing a form.

Open questions for you

1. Should prayer times live as a tab, or as a row on home (shown here)? A tab is more prominent but you have three already.

2. Default slots on first run — offer Morning/Midday/Evening pre-filled, or start empty and let people build their own?

3. How many grace days per week? One is shown. Two is more forgiving; zero makes it a plain streak.
