Prayer UI — round two

Two directions for the prayer screen plus a rebuilt history screen. Real lib/theme.ts colours, dark by default (toggle top-right). Nothing is built yet — pick a direction and I'll implement it.

0What was wrong

The week strip was a rolling 7-day window ending today — so it read T F S S M T W. Every letter was correct and the whole thing was unreadable, because a week that starts on a different day each time you open it isn't a week. Your Mood card already uses a fixed Sun–Sat calendar week. Two different week metaphors in one app is the actual bug.
History was a bare grid. No month navigation, no totals, nothing to feel. The Mood Calendar next door has month nav and a statistics row — prayer history had less than the feature it sits beside.

ADirection A — consistent with SermonMate

Fixed Sun–Sat week, matching the Mood card exactly. Today is ringed. Reuses patterns already in the app, so it feels native to SermonMate rather than imported.

Why this
Real dates, not just letters. Removes the ambiguity entirely — 29 is unmistakable in a way "W" is not.
Per-slot streaks are the strongest idea here: "Morning, streak 12 days" is more motivating than one global number, and it surfaces which habit is actually sticking.
Cost: per-slot streaks mean more computation, and a slot you delete and recreate loses its streak. It also nudges the app toward productivity-tool territory, away from the quiet companion framing you locked in.

CHistory — rebuilt

Month navigation and a statistics row, mirroring the Mood Calendar so the two screens are siblings. Calendar uses day numbers with a dot beneath — the same language the Mood Calendar already speaks — instead of filled blocks.

Why this
Dots under numbers instead of filled blocks — exactly what your Mood Calendar does. The two history screens become siblings instead of strangers.
Best streak is the number that keeps people going after a break. Current streak alone punishes a fresh start; "best 21" says the 21 still happened.
Month navigation makes the past reachable. Without it the screen only ever shows now, which is the opposite of a history.
Missed days stay blank. No red anywhere on this screen.

My recommendation

Direction A for the prayer screen, plus C for history. A costs nothing new and makes the two week-strips in your app agree, which is the actual complaint. B is the better-looking screen in isolation, but per-slot streaks add real state and pull the product toward habit-tracker rather than companion — and the date strip duplicates what history is for.

If you want one thing from B, take the date numbers in the week strip (Sun 26, Mon 27 …) instead of bare letters. That kills the ambiguity for free and does not change any data.
